Privacy Policy for Pilot Flashcard

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Pilot Flashcard respects your privacy. The app does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from its users.

Information We Collect

Pilot Flashcard does not require an account and does not collect personal data such as your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

In-App Purchases

If you purchase access to paid content, the transaction is processed by Apple through the App Store. Pilot Flashcard does not receive or store your payment card information. The app may use Apple's StoreKit system to determine whether purchased content should be unlocked on your device.

Analytics and Tracking

Pilot Flashcard does not use third-party advertising, tracking, or analytics services.

Children's Privacy

Pilot Flashcard does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the app's features or data practices change. Any updates will be posted on this page.

Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or the app, please visit the Pilot Flashcard Support page.
